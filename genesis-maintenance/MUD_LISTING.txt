DESCRIPTION

Genesis Reforged is a class-driven, GodWars-derived MUD for players who
enjoy supernatural powers, distinct combat styles and character development.
Vampire disciplines, Werewolf gifts, martial arts, arcane magic and cybernetic
components offer different approaches to building a character.

The current build includes 19 standalone classes:

Angel, Cyborg, Deathknight, Demon, Dragon, Drow, Elemental, Gargoyle,
Highlander, Kuei-jin, Mage, Monk, Moogle, Ninja, Phoenix, Sorcerer,
Vampire, Werewolf and Wraith.

Magic has more than one path. Mages develop their spheres, while Sorcerer
book mages write, bind and chant spells. Sorcerers have starter-grimoire
support with introductory spells and instructions for making more.
The Chaos book-mage variant adds changing page colors while preserving
the page's spell role.

Werewolves develop breed, auspice and tribe gifts. Vampires explore
disciplines and supernatural forms. Monks use arts, techniques and mantras;
Ninjas develop their principles. Cyborgs bring a component-based kit to
a roster that also includes Angels, Demons, Dragons and Phoenixes.

Class-specific powers, armor and weapons, fighting stances, and expanded
in-game class and ability help support those different play styles.
Maintenance work includes targeted repairs to legacy spell effects,
ability costs, travel restrictions and character-state handling.

DEVELOPMENT NOTE

The current build focuses on standalone class play. A roster of 171 named
two-class hybrids exists as a read-only preview. Hybrid conversion,
combined ability inheritance and pure-class ascension are not active
features and should not be listed as available gameplay yet.
